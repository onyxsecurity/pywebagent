=================== 1. THE RENDER — read from the FINAL ConfigMap, not from spec.values ===================
    Two different questions were being conflated. What `spec.values` carries is not what the pod reads:
    the chart fills its own defaults in afterwards. Both are measured here.

    (a) spec.values, built by kustomize from the instance overlay:
        $ kustomize build --load-restrictor LoadRestrictionsNone \
            clusters/ox-az-p-eus2-c02/instances/<inst>/onyx-app
          | yq '…HelmRelease onyx-app .spec.values."endpoint-asset-ingestion".configMap.data'
        has("asset_ingestion_sqs")  ->  false     on BOTH instances
        The earlier `null` came from asking yq for that missing key: `yq '.asset_ingestion_sqs'` on a map
        without it prints `null`. That is the query's answer, not the render's.
        The key is absent because drop-aws-only-values.yaml removes it — deliberately with `op: remove`
        and NOT `op: add … value: null`, and that file's own comment says why in as many words: add-null
        "SURVIVES into the rendered ConfigMap, so it would emit `asset_ingestion_sqs: null` into
        /app/config.yaml … and the pod crash-loops on boot".

    (b) the FINAL config.yaml the pod mounts, rendered through the chart:
        $ helm template onyx-app infra/helm/onyx-app -f <the spec.values above>
          | yq 'select(.kind=="ConfigMap" and name~"endpoint-asset-ingestion") | .data["config.yaml"]'

        asset_ingestion_sqs:
              aws_region: ""
              dlq_url: ""
              endpoint: ""
              queue_url: ""
              visibility_timeout: 600
              wait_time_seconds: 20
        asset_ingestion_s3:
              aws_region: ""
              endpoint: ""

        key present in the shipped config? true          us-east-1 occurrences in the lane block: 0
        Identical on saas and centerpoint.

    So the honest statement is: `asset_ingestion_sqs` is REMOVED from spec.values and reappears as the
    chart's all-blank stanza in the ConfigMap — blank on every leaf, never null. `asset_ingestion_s3` is
    the opposite case and is blanked in release.yaml instead, because ITS chart default is a live
    `aws_region: "us-east-1"` (infra/helm/onyx-app/values.yaml:2485) that a remove would let back in.
