THE RESOLUTION, AND THE CORRECTED SENTENCE FOR THE PR BODY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The PR body and the previous artifact were not measuring the same identity. Both readings are real:

    identity                                      SEND      LISTEN
    --------------------------------------------  --------  --------
    authored endpoint-asset-ingestion role set     ALLOWED   DENIED     <- what the PR body meant
    coder-agent-app (lab runtime, superset)        ALLOWED   ALLOWED    <- what was measured at verify
    the two roles under test, alone                DENIED    DENIED     <- why grant 3 exists

The body's sentence is true of the AUTHORED identity and false of the identity the lab was running.
It must therefore name the identity. Paste this:

    On `alert-processing-prdct11935` the authored endpoint-asset-ingestion identity gets SEND and
    not LISTEN: the queue-scoped `Azure Service Bus Data Sender` supplies the Send claim, and with
    no Receiver at that scope a receive is refused with `'Listen' claim(s) are required`. Measured
    live on a purpose-built identity holding exactly the authored grants. The lab's previous runtime
    identity (`coder-agent-app`) shows LISTEN as *allowed* only because it separately holds a
    namespace-wide `Azure Service Bus Data Receiver` on `prdct11935bus`, which the module never

                      "aud":["api://AzureADTokenExchange"]}

    First attempt, subject deliberately not yet matching — Entra reaches the RECORD lookup:
      AADSTS700213: No matching federated identity record found for presented assertion subject
      'system:serviceaccount:onyxd:at9lp-fed-probe'.

the OIDC issuer is unavailable. Ownership: it can be proven on any cluster with a public OIDC issuer
(AKS with --enable-oidc-issuer, or a self-managed issuer published to a public HTTPS endpoint).
NOTE what was NOT done to paper over it: no superset credential was substituted anywhere, and the
runtime identity in §2/§3 is secretless precisely so no client secret had to stand in for federation.
