## 2. WHY A DEDICATED, LEAST-PRIVILEGE IDENTITY COULD NOT BE MINTED HERE
    $ az ad sp create-for-rbac --name prdct11935-eai-dedicated-probe --role Reader --scopes <rg>
        ERROR: Insufficient privileges to complete the operation.
    The agent identity cannot create service principals in this tenant, so the lane has to borrow
    the lab's admin SP. That is a LAB limitation, not a property of the shipped design.

## 3. WHAT THE SHIPPED DESIGN ACTUALLY GRANTS — the TF, which is what merges
    modules/onyx/cell-azure-instance/workload_identity.tf gives the endpoint-asset-ingestion
    workload identity exactly three queue/account-scoped data roles and nothing else:
      · Azure Service Bus Data Receiver  — on ITS OWN ingestion queue
      · Azure Service Bus Data Sender    — on the alert-processing queue (added this round; without
                                            it the sessions fan-out cannot publish at all — at11-01)
      · Storage Blob Data Reader         — on the ingestion storage account
    No namespace-wide grant, no Contributor, no Owner, no subscription-scoped role.

## 4. THE SUBSTANCE STILL PROVES OUT, and here is the sharpest form of it
    Control-plane breadth does NOT buy data-plane access: on ONE real queue, with the identity
    holding subscription User Access Administrator and RG Contributor, the two actions diverge
    exactly as the role assignments say they should.
    SEND  on alert-processing-prdct11935: ALLOWED  (the queue-scoped Data Sender grant this PR adds)
    LISTEN on alert-processing-prdct11935: DENIED — the Listen claim is required
       verbatim: Unauthorized access. 'Listen' claim(s) are required to perform this operation. Resource: 'sb://prdct11935bus.servicebus.windows.net/alert-processing-prdct11935'. TrackingId:7bb3cb3388074235a

    That is the property at9 exists to demonstrate — Service Bus data-plane authorisation is
    per-queue and per-action, and the broad control-plane roles the lab SP carries do not satisfy it.

## 5. THE ESCALATION — what remains unproven on this box, and who owns it
    NOT PROVEN HERE: that the SHIPPED workload identity, in a real cell, holds only the three grants in
    section 3. What is proven is (a) the Terraform that creates it grants exactly those, and (b) the
    data-plane enforcement those grants rely on is real, measured in section 4.
    OWNER: setup, if a dedicated SP can be provisioned in the agent tenant; otherwise this stays a
    stated lab deviation and the cell's own deployment is the place it is verified.
    NOT DONE INSTEAD: the claim was not quietly narrowed, and the over-privilege was not left implied by
    a parenthetical. The PR body now states the lab footprint plainly.
