## NOTE ON THE TWO BOUNDARY RULES, stated so the classification is auditable rather than felt:
##   IN if the instrument's Labels class carries a disposition field (outcome/reason/result/verdict/
##      write/lane/kind/source), OR the instrument's own name records a unit being dropped, skipped,
##      parked or retried (skipped|dropped|failures|dead_letters|poison|retries). The second rule exists
##      because this inventory's real boundary was always wider than "has a disposition label" —
##      `agents_reingest_skipped` and `dead_letters` are carried on exactly that basis, which is why
##      `agents_skipped` belonged here all along.
##   OUT for histograms (duration/size), gauges (a level, not a disposition), volume counters with no
##      disposition field, and instruments declared but never emitted in these roots (reported as gaps).
